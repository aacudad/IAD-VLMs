Trace Curation / Self-Training Pipeline

Teaching the model to fix its own mistakes

The headline RL model (SFT+GRPO run‑2) is rolled out on 4,236 held‑out images it never trained on. A local reward scores each attempt; a Gemini judge then re‑checks the ones that look fine; anything wrong or borderline gets corrected or rewritten by Gemini before it's fed back into the next training round. This is the self‑distillation loop behind iteration‑2 / Variety‑STaR.

The pipeline

Six stages, run end to end by run_pipeline_heldout4k.sh — identical thresholds and prompts to the 10,236‑item in‑distribution run.

Stage A
phase0_rollout_kscoring.py

Stage B

Stage C

Gemini faithfulness judge re‑checks the "good" bucket (keep_threshold=2) — some get downgraded to needs‑rewrite.

Stage E
phase1b_gemini_correct.py

phase1b_gemini_correct.py

What happened to the 4,236 rollouts

Each item's chosen rollout ends up in exactly one of three states.

4,236 held‑out items
The source (docs/heldout_4k_rollout.md) reports these as ≈4,236 in total and notes needs‑rewrite is a "local + judge union" — a small number of items move from the initial good bucket (3,673) into needs‑rewrite after Gemini's faithfulness pass (which is why "good, faithfulness‑confirmed" separately reads 3,589 / 84.7%). Shown here as the source's own final disposition, not independently re‑partitioned.
This matches the in‑distribution training pool. The 10,236‑item pool used for the original SFT+GRPO training buckets the same way — 8,872 good, 86.7% — meaning the model's rollout quality on held‑out, never‑seen images is statistically indistinguishable from its quality on images it trained on.

How the teacher decides: fix vs. rewrite

Two different Gemini system prompts, chosen by which bucket an item lands in. Both are reference‑guided (image + GT mask + GT type/location) and both default to the smallest edit that works — full rewrite is the fallback, not the default.

Correct
for needs_correction — the trace's conclusion is wrong
Treat the failed attempt as the baseline. Keep every sentence that's factually correct about the image, even though the conclusion is wrong. Edit only what must change to reach the right answer — flip the conclusion, fix one contradicting claim, or add one or two sentences describing a missed defect at the GT location. The <think> block may not grow more than 30% versus the original.
What changes
"no contamination visible"→flip the one sentence that contradicts the visible mark
missed defect→add 1–2 sentences at the GT location, same prose style
Rewrite
for needs_rewrite — conclusion is right, grounding is weak
The trace already reaches the correct answer. Make surgical edits by default: keep nearly all of the original prose, and only touch the specific words that are vague or speculative. Escalate to a full rewrite only if the input is fundamentally broken — wrong camera view, invented components, hallucinated geometry. The output <think> block may not be longer than the input.
What changes (real edit examples from the prompt)
"the red mark"→"the bright red smudge" (only if visible)
"I see a deep scratch"→"I see a fine scratch" (only if depth was wrong)
"this could mean a manufacturing issue"→deleted (speculation, not observation)
